Exam Instructions

General Rules:

  • The exam will run in fullscreen mode. Do not exit fullscreen.
  • Do not switch tabs or windows — this will be detected and recorded.
  • Right-click, copy, paste, and keyboard shortcuts are disabled.
  • Developer tools (F12, Ctrl+Shift+I) are blocked and tracked.
  • The timer is synchronized with the server. Time will not pause.
  • If you log in late, you will get only the remaining time.
  • Your answers are saved automatically as you select them.
  • Click "Submit Exam" when done. Auto-submit happens when time ends.
  • Once submitted, you cannot retake the exam.
Warning: After 3 security violations (tab switch, fullscreen exit, dev tools, etc.) your registration will be auto-suspended and you cannot continue the exam.

Questions will be displayed in the selected language. Once selected, language cannot be changed during the exam.
